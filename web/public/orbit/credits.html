Orbit data credits

Lunar surface

Ruslan Kabatsayev created the global lunar colour map from LROC WAC imagery for Stellarium. The Orbit textures are tone-adjusted, resized WebP adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0. Original texture · Licence

Global relief and polar elevation and reflectance come from NASA’s LRO Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter team. The polar elevation maps have a source resolution of 240 m. Terrain shadows are approximate. LOLA products

Earth, Sun and time

Earth uses the Blue Marble composite by Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon, NASA Earth Observatory. NASA Blue Marble

Sun and Earth directions are sampled hourly from the project’s NAIF SPICE kernels, including DE440s, in the lunar mean-Earth frame (MOON_ME_DE440_ME421, CN+S). Earth orientation uses ITRF93. The browser interpolates these samples between 2026 and 2030. The Sun and Earth disks indicate sky directions at illustrative sizes. NASA NAIF
